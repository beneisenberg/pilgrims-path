# ⛪ Pilgrim's Path

*A cute 2D pixel-art study game for mastering the Church History I mid-term — from Paul's conversion (35 A.D.) to the Council of Chalcedon (451 A.D.).*

Play it here: **https://beneisenberg.github.io/pilgrims-path/**

Walk the road of early church history as a young novice (accompanied by Balaam, a deeply sarcastic pack mule), and turn dates, people, heresies, and lesson-review questions into muscle memory. The whole thing is a single self-contained HTML file — no installation, no accounts, no build step.

---

## ✨ Features

- **📖 Story Mode — "The Road to Chalcedon"** — a five-chapter narrated journey with character portraits, mastery gates, wayside inns, optional side-quests, boss battles, and a healthy dose of ironic humor. Reaching Chalcedon unlocks a full mock **Mid-Term** exam.
- **🗓️ Timeline Trek** — match events to their years, with a *Scholar* mode that uses nearby-year decoys (144 vs. 150 vs. 155) to drill the exact confusions the exam tests.
- **🛤️ Order the Ages** — place shuffled events in chronological order, then watch the pilgrim walk your answer down the road.
- **👤 Saints & Scholars** — "Who am I?" clue-by-clue identification of 23 historical figures.
- **👹 Heresy Hunter** — nine boss battles against Celsus, the Gnostics, Marcion, Arius, Apollinaris, Eutyches, and more, ending with the Examiner.
- **📜 Catechism Class** — the lesson-review questions drilled unit by unit.
- **⚡ Lightning Vespers** — a 60-second rapid-fire round; hesitations get sent to your review queue.
- **🔥 Daily Pilgrimage** — a short spaced-repetition session of exactly what you're about to forget, with a daily streak.
- **🃏 Scroll Match** — a memory-card game pairing events with dates.
- **📚 Study Chapel** — a quiet reference of every date, mnemonic, person, heresy, and council.
- **👘 Wardrobe & Achievements** — earn hats, robes, held accessories, and a pet dove as you play.
- **🎵 Original medieval soundtrack** with map and battle themes (toggleable).

## 🧠 How it helps you learn

The game is built around evidence-based study techniques rather than passive review:

- **Spaced repetition** — a Leitner-box system tracks every item and resurfaces it right before you'd forget it. Missed questions cycle back within the same session, and weak items feed the Daily Pilgrimage.
- **Active recall** — every mode is a retrieval exercise, not a read-through.
- **Interleaving** — dates, people, and review questions are mixed rather than blocked.
- **Dual coding** — a proportional visual timeline anchors each date in space as well as time.
- **Bidirectional cues** — you're asked event→year *and* year→event, so the knowledge works from any angle.
- **Immediate feedback** — every answer shows the correct response plus a memory hook, and waits for you to read it before moving on.

## 💾 Saving your progress

Progress is saved automatically in your browser's local storage — no login required.

- Each player's progress is **private to their own browser and device**.
- Switching devices (phone ↔ laptop) starts a fresh save on the new device.
- Clearing your browser data for the site will erase your progress.

## 📱 Install as an app

On a phone, open the game in your browser, then choose **"Add to Home Screen"** from the browser menu. You'll get an app icon that launches the game fullscreen — no app store needed.

## 🕹️ Running it locally

Because it's a single HTML file, you can just download `index.html` and double-click it to open in any modern browser. That's it.

## 🛠️ Built with

Plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks, no dependencies. Graphics are hand-drawn pixel art rendered as inline SVG; sound and music run through the Web Audio API.
